Monthly scope

Agree the publishing operation before the first post.

Social media work is planned as a recurring service with defined channels, output volume, approvals and response boundaries. The monthly fee and deliverables appear in the agreement.

Plan and create

Content pillars, calendar, copy and platform-sized creative in an agreed monthly quantity. The client supplies brand facts, offer details and rights-cleared assets.

Review and publish

Drafts move through the agreed approval window before publishing. Accounts remain client-owned; access is granted through platform roles where available.

Respond and report

Community response hours, escalation triggers and supported message types are agreed in writing. Reporting uses available platform metrics and does not guarantee reach, leads or sales.

Clear boundaries

Protect account ownership and brand accuracy.

Ad spend, influencer fees, media buying, contests, crisis response, moderation outside agreed hours, photography and extra content are separate unless the proposal includes them.

The client remains responsible for the accuracy and legal approval of claims, prices, promotions and regulated-industry statements before publication.

Client approvals

Identify a decision-maker, backup approver and response time. Late feedback may move the calendar; unapproved content is not published unless the agreement sets another process.
