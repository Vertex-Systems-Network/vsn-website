Who we support

Individual brands and company marketing have different decision-makers, proof points and content risks. We shape the plan around the account owner, audience and business goal.

Make expertise easier to discover.

  • Voice and topic discovery
  • Thought-leadership posts and profile updates
  • Launch, speaking or announcement support
  • Clear approval for personal statements and images
For businesses

Connect channel activity to operations.

For a business, product or service page, we map content to the customer journey and coordinate campaigns with the website, store, sales team or support process where relevant.

More than social media

Reach the right audience across the channels that fit.

We can combine profile management with search visibility, paid campaigns, content and conversion work. The channel mix starts with audience intent, market and available budget—not a fixed bundle of platforms.

Search engine optimisation

Review technical foundations, search intent, page structure, local discovery, internal links and content opportunities. SEO work can include technical and on-page recommendations, keyword-to-page planning, location pages, metadata and performance reporting. Rankings and traffic are not guaranteed.

Paid search and social advertising

Plan and manage agreed Google Search, Shopping, Display or YouTube campaigns and paid campaigns on channels such as Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok or Pinterest where the audience, account and market support them. Scope defines campaign objectives, targeting, creative, landing destination, tracking, tests and review schedule.

Landing pages and conversion paths

Improve the route from campaign to action through message alignment, page structure, calls to action, lead forms and analytics events. Conversion-rate experiments are included only when traffic, tooling, privacy settings and the approved test plan support a useful read.

Content strategy and production

Turn business knowledge into planned social posts, articles, short-form video concepts, campaign copy, email content and landing-page messaging. The scope specifies formats, quantities, revisions, source materials, rights-cleared assets, approvals and publishing responsibilities.

Know where the media budget goes.

Ad spend is paid to the platform through client-owned accounts and is separate from VSN’s management fee unless a signed proposal states otherwise. Platform access, audience eligibility, tracking consent, bid systems and third-party availability affect delivery. Reports distinguish media spend from service fees and focus on agreed business measures; they do not promise sales, leads or return on ad spend.

Campaign operations

Every campaign starts with a brief and a decision.

Publishing regularly and running a campaign are related but different jobs. Campaign work has a defined audience, offer, schedule, asset set and review point before it goes live.

Set the objective

Choose the intended next action—awareness, qualified enquiries, registrations, store visits or sales—and agree how it can be observed.

Understand the audience

Review the audience, market, customer questions, current channel evidence and any client-provided research.

Shape the message

Agree the offer, proof, tone, call to action, destination page and any claims that need client or specialist approval.

Prepare the assets

Produce or adapt approved copy and creative for the selected placement, format and campaign schedule.

Launch and monitor

Publish after approval. If paid management is included, monitor the agreed account, spend limits and optimisation rules.

Review the evidence

Compare results with the agreed baseline and objective, record what was learned, then recommend the next test or adjustment.

Advertising outcomes depend on the offer, audience, platform, landing experience, seasonality, budget and available data. VSN does not promise follower counts, reach, leads, sales or return on ad spend.

Email marketing

Use email to continue a relevant conversation.

Email supports relationships beyond a social feed. We can plan campaign broadcasts and, where the client’s platform and data allow, set up lifecycle messages such as welcome, onboarding, event follow-up or post-purchase education.

Work may include campaign themes, audience segmentation, copy and design coordination, send scheduling, testing, automation maps and reporting. The actual platform, number of sends, flows, revisions and list work are agreed in the proposal.

Permission and deliverability matter

Client-approved, permission-based lists are the starting point. We do not build campaigns around scraped or purchased recipient lists. The client remains responsible for the lawful basis, source and accuracy of its audience data and for approving the final message.

  • Respect opt-outs and suppression requests
  • Review sender identity and domain authentication with the client
  • Check links, rendering and tracking before scheduled sends
  • Use an approved email service provider and client-owned account

Email platform fees, list acquisition, CRM work and advanced integrations are separate unless written into scope.

Monthly operating rhythm

Know what is planned, waiting and published.

A shared calendar and approval process make responsibilities visible. This helps avoid rushed claims, missing assets and campaigns launching without a clear owner.

Plan

Set the month’s themes, key dates, priority channels, campaigns and required client inputs.

Create

Draft copy and prepare creative in the agreed formats and monthly quantities.

Approve

Collect consolidated feedback from the named approver within the agreed review window.

Publish

Schedule or publish approved assets using client-owned accounts and available platform roles.

Engage

Handle agreed interactions or route messages according to the response and escalation guide.

Learn

Review channel signals, campaign outcomes and content patterns; use findings to shape the next plan.

Measurement

Report the numbers that answer the business question.

Reporting is selected to fit the goal and the data that each platform or email provider can reliably supply. The report can distinguish activity from outcomes, so posting volume is not presented as proof of growth.

Depending on the scope, the review may include reach, engagement, saves, video retention, profile actions, qualified enquiries, landing-page activity, email clicks, unsubscribes, conversions or campaign spend.

Decision-ready review

Each reporting cycle should answer: what was planned, what shipped, what changed, what the evidence can support, and what should be tested next. Attribution gaps and platform limitations are called out instead of filled with assumptions.

Accountability and access

Keep ownership, approvals and access clear.

Marketing can speak for an individual or a company. Account control and approval rights must therefore be agreed before publishing or campaign work begins.

Client-owned accounts

Social profiles, advertising accounts, domains, email audiences and sending platforms remain under client ownership. VSN uses delegated roles where the platform supports them; clients retain recovery and billing control.

Approval before publication

The client names an approver for posts, offers, personal claims, pricing, regulated statements and campaign budgets. Late or missing approvals can move publication dates.

Defined service limits

Photography, influencer fees, ad spend, inbox coverage outside agreed hours, crisis response, legal review, list migration and custom integrations are excluded unless the written proposal includes them.

Please do not send account passwords, recovery codes, customer exports or other sensitive records through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. Access is arranged through approved platform permissions during onboarding.

Common questions

What to expect from a managed growth service.

Scope is tailored to the channels, audience, workload and decisions your team needs to support.

Do you provide SEO and Google Ads as well as social media?

Yes, an engagement can include technical or on-page SEO, local search work, Google Ads and agreed paid social channels. The channel mix, deliverables, account access, reporting measures and media budget are written into the scope.

Is advertising spend included in the management fee?

Platform spend is normally paid by the client through their own advertising account and is separate from VSN’s management fee. The proposal will show the service fee, media budget assumptions and any additional production or platform costs separately.

Can you manage an individual profile as well as a company page?

Yes. The proposal can cover an individual professional or personal brand, a business page, or both. The account owner approves positioning, profile updates and all posts that represent them.

Which social platforms can you support?

Platform selection depends on where the intended audience is active and what the team can sustain well. Channels such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Snapchat and X can be considered where the audience, account access and market support them, then listed in the agreed scope.

Do you create and publish posts?

Content planning, copy, creative coordination, scheduling and publishing can be included in the monthly quantity. The proposal specifies formats, revisions, client-supplied assets and the approval deadline.

Can you run paid social campaigns?

Campaign planning and account management can be scoped. The client controls the ad account and pays the platform directly; ad spend is separate from VSN’s management fee unless a proposal explicitly says otherwise.

What email marketing can you provide?

Depending on the client’s email platform and audience, work may include newsletters, promotional campaigns, audience segments, template coordination, testing, reporting and agreed automation flows. Platform charges and migration or integration work are scoped separately.

Can you work with an existing subscriber list?

Yes, after the client confirms how the list was collected and which audiences may be contacted. We work from client-approved, permission-based lists, respect suppression and unsubscribe requests, and do not use scraped or purchased recipient lists.

How do you measure growth?

We agree measures that match the goal and available data. Reports may include reach and engagement, profile actions, qualified enquiries, email clicks, conversions or spend; they explain attribution limits and do not promise a specific result.

Do you need our account passwords?

Account ownership stays with you. Where possible, access is provided through platform roles or delegated permissions. Do not send passwords or recovery codes through WhatsApp or the enquiry form.

What is included in the monthly fee?

The proposal lists channels, content quantity and formats, approvals, community coverage, campaign workload, reporting cadence and any included email sends or automations. Media spend, software licences and other third-party costs are identified separately.
