Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · Version 1.2

Website operator: Vertex Systems Network (Private) Limited · SECP 0313834 · NTN H979166 · Islamabad Capital Territory, Pakistan · info@vertexsystemsnetwork.com.

This policy describes the default cancellation and refund approach for VSN services. A signed proposal, SOW, subscription agreement, marketplace policy or order form may set different rules for a specific engagement.

1. Before substantive work begins

If a custom project is cancelled before substantive work begins, VSN may refund eligible prepaid fees after deducting non-recoverable payment-processing charges and third-party costs where permitted by law.

2. Work already performed

Discovery, consulting, content, design, development, setup and other completed work may be charged under the agreed milestone or rate. An eligible refund is assessed against the undelivered portion, subject to the agreement and mandatory rights.

3. Milestones and acceptance

A milestone that has been delivered and accepted, or that has passed an agreed acceptance period without a documented material defect, is normally non-refundable except where required by law or the applicable signed agreement.

Profile website package fees

Published profile prices are starting fees, not unconditional deposits. If a project is cancelled, completed milestones and committed costs are reconciled against the uncompleted scope under the proposal.

4. Third-party costs

Domains, hosting, themes, extensions, platform plans, model/API usage, payment charges, advertising spend and other third-party purchases may be non-refundable once committed under the provider’s rules.

5. Monthly retainers and BPO

Social media, BPO, support and maintenance retainers may reserve staff capacity and are billed under the agreed period. Cancellation affects future periods according to the notice period, service already performed and reconciliation terms in the agreement.

Company setup and business operations

Company setup coordination may include separate work stages such as route assessment, document preparation, authority submission support and post-setup handover. Fees for completed work are reconciled against the undelivered portion under the signed agreement. Government, registry, bank, professional and other third-party charges are treated according to the relevant provider or authority rules and are not represented as refundable by VSN.

Tax and compliance services

Completed document review, preparation or filing coordination is handled under the engagement. Government charges and third-party professional fees may not be refundable. No refund can guarantee or reverse a tax-authority decision.

6. SaaS subscriptions

VSN SaaS products may be billed directly or through a marketplace. The billing and refund policy shown at the point of purchase or by the marketplace controls. Cancelling a subscription normally stops future renewal; access may continue until the end of the paid period unless the product terms state otherwise.

7. Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe a charge was duplicated or billed incorrectly, contact VSN promptly with the invoice or transaction reference so the charge can be reviewed.

8. Material delivery failure

If VSN materially fails to deliver an agreed item and does not cure the issue within a reasonable period after written notice, the applicable agreement determines the remedy for the affected undelivered portion.

9. Requesting a cancellation or refund

Submit the request through the VSN contact page with the project, invoice or order reference and the reason for the request. VSN may ask for information reasonably needed to investigate the matter.

Product and service cancellation are handled separately.

Custom software, website and profile work is reconciled against completed milestones and committed costs in the signed proposal. Social media, BPO, maintenance and other reserved monthly capacity follow their stated notice period and work already performed. SaaS renewal cancellation stops future billing under the applicable subscription terms; access and any refund follow those terms or the marketplace checkout rules. Tax coordination fees and government or professional charges are assessed under the engagement and provider rules. Applicable mandatory consumer rights are not excluded by this policy.
