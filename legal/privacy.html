Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertex Systems Network (Private) Limited (“VSN”) handles personal information through this corporate website and general service inquiries. Product-specific privacy notices may apply to individual VSN SaaS products.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with VSN, we may receive your name, business contact details, company information, project requirements, communications, billing records, support information and technical information such as browser, device or security logs generated by the services you use.

2. How we use information

VSN may use information to respond to inquiries, scope and deliver services, manage client relationships, issue invoices, provide support, secure systems, prevent abuse, maintain business records, improve services and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Website project form

The current static project form prepares the information you enter for WhatsApp and does not intentionally submit the form contents to a VSN website database. If you continue in WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to the terms and privacy practices of that messaging service.

4. Payments

When VSN uses a payment processor, bank or hosted payment page, payment information is processed by that provider under its own privacy and security terms. VSN should not receive or store full card numbers through this website.

5. Service providers

VSN may use trusted providers for hosting, cloud infrastructure, communications, analytics, development, support and payments. Providers should receive only the information reasonably necessary for their role and may process it in other countries.

6. Retention

VSN keeps information for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, including active service delivery, contractual records, tax/accounting requirements, security investigations and dispute handling. Retention periods can differ by service and jurisdiction.

7. Security

VSN uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service, which may include role-based access, multi-factor authentication, secure development practices, backups and least-privilege access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. International processing

VSN is based in Pakistan and may work with clients and service providers in other countries. Information may therefore be processed across borders where permitted and necessary for service delivery.

9. Your choices and requests

Where applicable, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by VSN. Some records may need to be retained for legal, contractual, accounting or security reasons. Requests can be initiated through the contact page.

10. Children

VSN’s corporate services are intended for businesses and professional customers. The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through account creation or direct online checkout.

11. Updates

The effective date above identifies the current version of this Policy. Product-specific privacy notices may contain additional information.
