Responsible delivery

Data, models and operating costs are part of scope.

The proposal identifies approved data sources, model or API dependencies, expected usage assumptions, logging needs and who monitors the workflow. External model providers apply their own terms and data practices.

AI output may be incomplete or wrong. VSN does not promise autonomous accuracy or a business result; clients review outputs and approve actions appropriate to their operation.

Before sensitive data is used

Confirm lawful authority, access permissions, retention, provider settings and any required client or user notice. Do not send confidential or regulated records to a model unless the engagement explicitly authorizes that processing.
