Operating model

Move a repeatable process with its controls.

Every BPO engagement defines the tasks, working hours, service levels, escalation owner and systems access before VSN begins handling recurring work.

Customer support

Ticket, email or workflow-based first-line support, with approved response guidance, handoff rules and escalation paths.

Back-office operations

Documented data entry, record checks, administrative processing or recurring reporting with agreed quality checks.

Dedicated capacity

Roles, coverage windows, workload assumptions, client dependencies and continuity arrangements are described in a statement of work.

Security and accountability

Keep authority with the right account owner.

Before access is granted, the parties agree approved systems, least-privilege roles, identity checks, incident reporting and how access is removed at exit. Sensitive data handling and any required processing terms are agreed for the actual workflow.

Service levels depend on agreed staffing, volume, hours, client materials and systems availability. VSN does not promise performance outside the written operating scope.

Client responsibilities

Provide accurate procedures, escalation contacts, access approvals, training materials and timely decisions. A master agreement, statement of work, service-level schedule and confidentiality or data terms may be needed for the engagement.
