Three service modules

Start with setup, add launch support, then choose ongoing operations.

Modules can be purchased individually or combined. The proposal identifies the legal entity, owners, responsible officers, deliverables, service providers, authority limits, fees and client decisions before work starts.

Module 1 · Establish

Company Setup & Compliance Coordination

Assess a suitable Pakistan presence with the client and their qualified legal/tax advisers, then coordinate the approved company registration and initial tax-registration workflow.

  • Compare a new Pakistan company with a foreign-parent branch or liaison route, where relevant
  • Name and incorporation document checklist; application preparation/coordination where VSN is authorized

Module 2 · Launch

Pakistan Market Launch & Digital Foundation

Prepare the operating basics and customer-facing systems that help a properly established business present itself and begin work in Pakistan.

Managed Business Operations

Delegate the operational tasks VSN is engaged and equipped to perform, while the legal entity’s directors and authorized officers retain their formal responsibilities.

  • Monthly operating review, issue log and decision register for client approval
Choose your operating model

You decide what VSN handles and what your team runs.

Company ownership and operational support are separate decisions. A service-provider mandate does not make VSN the owner, director, employer of record, bank signatory, tax agent or legal representative unless a separate lawful arrangement explicitly establishes that role.

Client-owned entity

The client’s actual shareholders, directors and beneficial owners are disclosed in official records as applicable. VSN can help prepare and coordinate the approved setup process; the client retains ownership and statutory decisions.

Existing foreign parent / Pakistan presence

Assess whether a local subsidiary, branch or liaison-office route fits the intended activity. Foreign-company presence can involve prior permissions and SECP registration requirements; eligibility and filings need current legal review.

Client-managed after setup

VSN helps create the checklist, website, systems and handover pack. The client hires, files, approves contracts and operates the business through its own authorized people and advisers.

Delegated operational support

VSN performs only the contracted administrative, technology, staffing or customer-operation tasks. Any representative authority, signing mandate, handling of funds or regulated work must be lawful, specific, documented and accepted before service begins.

Joint venture or VSN-owned venture

This is considered only as a genuine commercial arrangement with real investment, documented rights, accurate ownership/control disclosures, conflict review and independent professional advice. It is not a nominee or placeholder ownership service.

Build-with-you launch

Client and VSN teams share project work under a responsibility matrix, approval flow and milestone plan. The client keeps company accounts, registrar credentials and final authority unless lawfully delegated.

Pakistan setup checkpoints

Registration is a sequence of authority-specific steps.

SECP describes company name reservation and incorporation as a formal company-formation process. FBR publishes separate income-tax registration requirements for companies, including company and authorized-officer information. A foreign company’s Pakistan place of business/branch or liaison route has a different documentary and permission pathway. These checks are not interchangeable, and completion or approval dates depend on the relevant authority and the case.

Depending on activity and location, additional provincial sales-tax, labour, social-security, industry licensing, data-protection, immigration or banking requirements may apply. VSN can help coordinate a checklist and service providers; legal opinions, statutory audits, licensed tax representation, regulated financial services and government approvals are not promised as part of a general setup package.

Ownership and authority safeguards

Do not put a company in another person’s name merely to hide the real owner or controller. SECP’s UBO framework focuses on identifying true natural-person owners/controllers in applicable cases. Any genuine VSN investment/JV must reflect real commercial rights and accurate disclosures.

Contracts, MOUs, filings and bank instructions are signed only by the entity’s authorized signatory or by a properly appointed representative acting within documented authority. VSN does not promise that a regulator, bank, landlord or counterparty will accept an application or agreement.

Common questions

Business setup and operations questions.

Entity choice, filings, fees and authority requirements depend on your specific facts and current Pakistani rules.

Can VSN register a Pakistan company for an overseas client?

We can scope setup coordination where legally available, with the true shareholders, directors, beneficial owners and authorized officers accurately identified. Eligibility and required documents depend on the founder, activity and current rules. Final applications are subject to authority review.

Can we put the company in VSN’s or another person’s name?

VSN does not provide nominee or placeholder ownership designed to conceal the real owner or controller. A VSN-owned venture or joint venture is considered only when VSN is a genuine investor with documented rights and accurate beneficial-ownership disclosures. Obtain independent legal advice before choosing ownership.

What is the difference between a Pakistan company and a branch office?

A locally incorporated company and a foreign company’s branch or liaison presence follow different legal, tax and approval pathways. The appropriate route depends on parent-company status, business activity and permissions. We can help organize a review with qualified advisers and current SECP/BOI requirements.

Can VSN sign an MOU, contract or application for us?

Only where the relevant entity has given lawful written authority to a named representative, and the document and activity permit that representation. Otherwise, the company’s authorized signatory signs. VSN will not sign as though it owns or controls a client business.

Do you provide company registration, tax filing and business operations together?

Yes. The three modules can be combined: setup and registration coordination, launch and digital foundation, and defined recurring operations. Each service, responsible professional and approval step is documented in the proposal.

Can you hire and manage our Pakistan team?

We can scope role design, recruitment coordination, onboarding, team coordination or selected HR administration. Employment, payroll and contractor arrangements must use the correct legal model, written agreements and applicable local registrations. VSN is not automatically the employer or employer of record.

Can you take care of our tax returns and recurring compliance?

We can organize a calendar, records workflow and tax-return preparation or filing coordination, subject to the client’s facts and agreed scope. Tax positions and submissions may require a qualified tax professional; due dates, filings and acceptance are confirmed against current official requirements.

Can we choose only the website, marketing or team module?

Yes. You may start with one module or one VSN service, such as web development, e-commerce, digital marketing or resource augmentation. Company incorporation is not required to buy those standalone services.

Do you guarantee registration, bank account opening, tax status or launch date?

No. Those decisions depend on regulators, banks, professional review, complete documents and third-party timelines. VSN can coordinate agreed tasks and provide status updates but cannot guarantee a decision or outcome outside its control.

What information should I include in the first enquiry?

Share the business activity, founder location, current entity status, preferred operating model, module and broad timeline. Do not send identity documents, tax IDs, bank records, passwords, employee files or signed contracts in the initial form.
