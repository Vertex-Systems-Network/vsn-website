Delivery evidence

What a future VSN case study must contain.

A case study should help a buyer evaluate delivery—not just decorate a website. VSN's publication standard is designed around traceable project context.

01

Problem

The actual business or product constraint the engagement was intended to solve.

02

Scope

What VSN was responsible for, and what was outside the engagement.

03

Delivery

Architecture, design decisions, integrations, milestones and QA approach where disclosure is permitted.

04

Outcome

Only measurable or client-approved results—never invented numbers or unsupported attribution.
