Billing & payments

Know the scope and recipient before you pay.

VSN invoices and proposals identify the contracting company, currency, taxes, payment milestones, due dates and approved payment route.

Use only details on a verified VSN invoice or a checkout link confirmed with info@vertexsystemsnetwork.com. Available methods depend on location and service.

Before making a payment

  • Confirm the invoice number and legal seller
  • Check currency, taxes, fees and schedule
  • Confirm whether domains, licences or APIs are included
  • Never send card details through chat or email
  • Keep your receipt and transaction reference
Different services, different billing

Your agreement explains the model.

Website & profile builds

May use a deposit and delivery milestones. Profile prices are starting fees; the proposal confirms the total, taxes and excluded costs.

Software & AI projects

Scoped through discovery and milestones. Cloud, API, model and licence costs are separate when identified.

Social media, BPO & support

Recurring work may be billed monthly for reserved capacity. Notice periods, hours and deliverables appear in the agreement.

VSN SaaS & marketplace products

Subscriptions, renewals, cancellations and refunds follow product or marketplace checkout terms.

Payment security: VSN does not request full card numbers, CVV or one-time passcodes by email or chat. Use hosted checkout or bank details on a verified invoice. Report suspected invoice changes to info@vertexsystemsnetwork.com.
