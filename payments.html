Commercial clarity

Payment should follow a written scope, not replace one.

VSN uses the payment method and schedule stated on the applicable proposal, order form or invoice. High-value custom projects are normally milestone-based.

Request a proposal →Refund policy

Bank transfer

Suitable for larger B2B invoices and milestone payments. Banking instructions should appear only on an official VSN invoice or verified payment communication.

Hosted card / payment link

Where activated, VSN can use a qualified payment provider so sensitive card details are entered on the provider’s secure checkout rather than stored on this website.

Marketplace billing

VSN SaaS sold through a marketplace may be billed by that marketplace under its own billing, tax and refund rules.

Typical billing

Billing model follows delivery risk.

These are operating models, not automatic terms for every engagement. The signed proposal or order form always defines the actual schedule.

01

Profile / small site

Commonly deposit + final payment before launch.

02

App / SaaS

Milestone billing tied to discovery, build, acceptance and launch.

03

BPO / support

Recurring retainer based on reserved capacity, staffing or SLA.

04

SaaS product

Recurring subscription or marketplace billing under product terms.

Payment safety

Verify before you pay.

Clients should use the beneficiary, currency and payment instructions shown on an official VSN invoice or verified checkout. VSN should never ask a customer to send full card details by email, chat or WhatsApp.

Before sending funds

  • Match the legal company name
  • Check the invoice / proposal reference
  • Verify changed bank details through a second channel
  • Use hosted checkout for card payments
  • Keep the transaction receipt
