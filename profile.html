A service for your personal brand

Your name. Your story. Your own professional home.

Authority Profile is a VSN service for individuals: founders, executives, consultants, creators, speakers and independent professionals. We build a personal website and supporting brand assets around the person whose name appears on the domain.

A company website explains an organization. Your personal profile connects your biography, expertise, work, publications and contact routes in one place that you control.

Built around the next opportunity

Every factual claim and image is reviewed by you before publication.

Delivery & ownership

A clear brief, review process and handover.

We start with your audience, field, public facts and visual preferences. You review the content direction before the build, then review the working profile before launch. Dates are confirmed after the content and access requirements are known.

The domain and hosting accounts should be in your name. You receive the agreed files and access after the contractual payment and handover steps. Third-party software remains subject to its licence.

What aftercare covers

Included aftercare begins at handover and covers small text, image or link updates within the monthly time allowance. Unused minutes do not roll over. New features, redesigns, third-party outages and platform subscription charges are outside that allowance.

One revision round means one consolidated list against the agreed direction. A new direction or added scope is priced before work continues.

Frequently asked questions

Before you build your personal brand.

Is Authority Profile for an individual or a company?

It is a service for a person's professional identity and personal website. Company websites, online stores and multi-person directories are scoped through our web-development services.

Do I need to write my biography first?

No finished biography is required. Essential starts with your supplied facts; Authority includes an interview; Signature includes two discovery sessions. All tiers require your approval of the final wording.

Does the package include translation?

It includes implementation of the stated number of language versions. You supply approved translations, or we quote translation and review separately.

Can I use real photos instead of AI portraits?

Yes. You can choose photo finishing using rights-cleared images. AI-assisted portraits are optional, use approved references and must not fabricate credentials, awards or attendance at events.

Do you guarantee Google rankings, AI mentions or press coverage?

No. We deliver the agreed content and technical setup. Search, AI and editorial platforms decide what they display. PR outreach is separately scoped and does not guarantee publication.

How do package changes and cancellation work?

The proposal confirms final fees, milestones, deliverables and third-party costs. Added work is quoted first. Cancellation is reconciled against completed work and committed costs under the Refund Policy.
