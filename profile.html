What the price covers

Scope, ownership and search claims.

The proposal lists pages, content, timeline, review period, payment milestones and handover.

Domain & hosting

Clients own their domain accounts. Hosting, domains, premium themes, fonts and plug-ins are separate unless listed in the proposal.

Content & approvals

Clients approve factual claims and provide rights-cleared images and credentials. Copywriting is limited to package scope.

Search foundations

Clear titles, headings and structured data can help machines interpret a page. Search and AI systems decide what they display.

Revisions & aftercare

Listed revision rounds apply before handover. New sections, redesigns and maintenance are quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

Do I own the domain and finished site?

Register the domain in your own account. Handover follows the proposal after payment; VSN keeps pre-existing reusable tools and third-party items follow their licences.

Can you guarantee a first-page or AI result?

No. VSN can create clear, structured content, but search engines and AI systems control indexing, rankings and citations.

Are package prices final for every project?

They are starting fees for the scope shown. The proposal confirms the total based on content, languages, integrations and other requirements.

Is translation included?

The package identifies supported languages. Translation is excluded unless the proposal includes it.

How do I request a package?

Email info@vertexsystemsnetwork.com with your role, package and preferred examples.
