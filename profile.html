FAQ

Before you start.

Do I own the domain and website?

For custom profile projects, the intended model is client ownership of the final custom deliverables after full payment, while third-party licenses and VSN pre-existing reusable components remain subject to their own terms.

Can you guarantee first-page Google or AI visibility?

No responsible provider should guarantee a specific ranking or AI citation. VSN can create strong technical and content foundations, but search and AI systems remain outside VSN's control.

Can the site be multilingual?

Yes. Multilingual scope can be included based on the package and content availability.
