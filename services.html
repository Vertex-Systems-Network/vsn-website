Before a proposal

What we need to scope the work.

The first conversation should produce a useful decision: an initial path, dependencies to confirm, and whether discovery is needed before an accurate price and timeline are possible.

Useful inputs

  • Your business goal and the people who will use the result.
  • Existing website, systems, content, data or operating process.
  • Required integrations, jurisdictions, languages and approvals.
  • Budget range, timing and who can make scope decisions.

We then confirm deliverables, exclusions, ownership and any third-party costs in a written proposal.
