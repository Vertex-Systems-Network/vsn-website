FAQs

Common questions before scoping.

These answers describe how VSN approaches an initial services conversation. A signed proposal remains the source of truth for any specific engagement.

Ask about your project →
Can VSN combine more than one service?+

Yes. A project can combine product engineering, web, AI, growth or operational support when those pieces belong to the same business outcome.

Do I need a complete technical brief first?+

No. Start with the goal, users, current systems, constraints and timing. VSN can turn that context into a clearer scope before implementation.

Can VSN work with an existing product or website?+

Yes. Existing code, platforms, integrations and operating constraints can be reviewed before deciding whether to extend, repair, migrate or replace anything.

What happens after the scope is agreed?+

The next step is a written delivery plan covering milestones, responsibilities, review points and the agreed commercial terms.
