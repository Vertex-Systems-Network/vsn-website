Store operations

A storefront includes the work behind checkout.

VSN scopes both the customer journey and the day-to-day tasks the store team must manage. A proposal confirms the platform, catalog size, integrations and launch responsibilities.

Catalog and merchandising

Product structure, collections, attributes, images and navigation organized so customers can compare and find items. Product facts, prices and rights-cleared media are supplied or approved by the client.

Checkout and integrations

Configure the agreed checkout experience, payment-provider connection and order notifications. Provider onboarding, eligibility, fees, fraud decisions and settlement remain controlled by the provider.

Order workflows

Connect store activity to approved inventory, CRM, fulfillment or accounting tools where supported. The scope documents which system is authoritative and how failed syncs are surfaced.

Launch readiness

Test the buying path before launch.

Agreed checks can cover product discovery, cart behavior, taxes and shipping configuration, transactional messages, mobile layouts and handoff to the store operator. Client approval and test credentials are required.

VSN does not guarantee sales, payment-provider approval, platform availability or compliance of the merchant's products and policies. Ongoing catalog work, support and platform fees are separate unless contracted.

Typical handover

Store access in client-owned accounts, a summary of configured features, any known limitations and the agreed launch checklist. Premium themes, paid apps, payment processing and advertising spend are identified separately.

A store you can operate

Assign the work behind every order.

A commerce build is only ready when the merchant knows who maintains products, answers customers, resolves failed payments and handles returns. The proposal separates build tasks from ongoing merchant operations.

Merchant decisions

Your business owns

Product accuracy, pricing, inventory, shipping and tax rules, consumer policies, payment-provider accounts and approval of customer communications. We can help organize this work where scoped.

Implementation

VSN can deliver

Store design and configuration, integrations, catalog migration, agreed automation, responsive checks and a handover explaining how your team uses the delivered system.

External systems

Providers control

Platform subscriptions, app licences, payment eligibility, transaction fees, carrier service and third-party API availability. These dependencies are identified before the launch plan is finalized.
