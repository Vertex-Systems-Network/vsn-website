For a reliable estimate

Tax depends on more than one number. A useful calculation may depend on taxpayer type, tax year, residency, income source, business structure, deductions, credits, tax already paid, withholding, assets and applicable federal or provincial rules.

Calculator outputs will be estimates using disclosed inputs and assumptions. A qualified professional should review a filing position when facts are incomplete, rules are unclear or a notice, dispute or representation is involved.

Common questions

What to know before starting.

Tax duties depend on the person, entity, income, period and current rules. A short intake helps identify the right work.

Can you file an individual return and a wealth statement?

We can scope preparation and filing coordination for individuals. Whether a wealth statement or other statement is required depends on the taxpayer’s circumstances and current filing rules.

Do you support freelancers and people with more than one income source?

Yes, we can organize a filing workflow for salary, freelance, professional, rental, investment or business income, subject to document review and the applicable rules for the tax year.

Do you handle company and business tax filings?

We can scope annual business or company returns, records, reconciliations, withholding and sales-tax coordination where applicable. The scope depends on entity type, accounting period, business activity and jurisdiction.

Will the calculators show the exact tax I owe?

No. They are intended to provide estimates using disclosed inputs and assumptions. Final liability can depend on facts, legal treatment, supporting records, current tax-year rules and authority review.

Are VSN’s calculators official FBR tools?

No. They are planned VSN tools informed by official sources. They will identify their source, tax year, assumptions and last-review date and will link users to the relevant official information.

Can you guarantee a refund, lower tax or acceptance by FBR?

No. Outcomes depend on the taxpayer’s facts, records, law and authority processing. VSN does not guarantee refunds, tax savings, return acceptance, active-taxpayer listing or notice outcomes.

Who gives formal advice or represents me?

Any formal advice, certification, appeal or representation that requires a qualified professional is handled only by the professional identified and agreed for that engagement.
