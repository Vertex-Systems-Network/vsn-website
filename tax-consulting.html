Preparation checklist

Bring records that support the numbers.

Use this as a planning list, not a request to send documents through the public enquiry form. The final document list depends on the selected tax year, return type and facts.

For salary earners and individuals

Start with employer salary certificates, bank profit certificates, rent or investment summaries, tax-deduction certificates, and a record of assets and liabilities when a wealth statement may be required. Keep supporting receipts for any claim that needs proof.

For freelancers and sole proprietors

Collect invoices, platform statements, client contracts, bank and payment-provider records, foreign-remittance evidence, expense invoices and withholding certificates. Separate business receipts from personal transfers and identify the currency and date for foreign receipts.

For companies and AOPs

Prepare the trial balance, financial statements, general ledger, bank reconciliations, payroll and vendor-payment records, tax certificates, prior returns and relevant registration details. The responsible reviewer confirms any additional schedules or entity documents.

For indirect-tax coordination

Keep sales invoices, credit/debit notes, purchase invoices, import records, registration details and filed-period data together. Match source records to the correct tax period before reconciling output tax, eligible input tax and payments.

Tax responsibilities by stage

Keep regular records so year-end work is not a scramble.

Small, consistent checks can make it easier to explain how a return was prepared and to respond when a figure does not match a certificate or statement.

At the time income is earned

Record the payer, date, gross amount, currency, income category and any tax withheld. For business receipts, link the entry to an invoice, contract or platform statement.

When a payment is made

Identify whether a withholding provision could apply before releasing payment. Keep the payment evidence, applicable section/rate reference and certificate or deposit record with the transaction.

At each return period

Reconcile invoices to books, output tax to sales records, input-tax claims to eligible purchase documents, and filed liabilities to payment receipts for the exact period.

Before annual filing

Compare the tax computation to final ledgers and certificates. Resolve unexplained differences, carry-forwards, missing records and any change in business or personal circumstances.

Official sources

Use the source that controls the relevant tax.

FBR guidance separates federal tax on goods from tax on services administered under federal ICT and provincial/territorial laws. A service must be classified for the right authority and location before a rate is applied.

  • FBR: filing an income-tax return and IRIS guidance
  • FBR: Income Tax Ordinance and amendments
  • FBR: Finance Act 2026
  • FBR: current withholding-tax rate cards, including Tax Year 2027
  • FBR: Sales Tax Act and amendments
  • FBR: sales-tax basics and goods / services scope
  • Punjab Revenue Authority
  • Sindh Revenue Board: taxable services
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority
  • Balochistan Revenue Authority

What needs a professional decision?

Calculations are only reliable after income and payment categories are classified. Review is especially important for mixed salary and business income, cross-border receipts, tax credits, final or minimum-tax treatment, loss carry-forward, related-party payments, disputed notices and provincial service-tax rules.

FBR publishes its rate card to help with estimates but says the amended statute prevails if the two conflict. The calculators therefore show assumptions and keep legally sensitive rates editable.

Common questions

What to know before starting.

Tax duties depend on the person, entity, income, period and current rules. A short intake helps identify the right work.

Can you file an individual return and a wealth statement?

We can scope preparation and filing coordination for individuals. Whether a wealth statement or other statement is required depends on the taxpayer’s circumstances and current filing rules.

Do you support freelancers and people with more than one income source?

Yes, we can organize a filing workflow for salary, freelance, professional, rental, investment or business income, subject to document review and the applicable rules for the tax year.

Do you handle company and business tax filings?

We can scope annual business or company returns, records, reconciliations, withholding and sales-tax coordination where applicable. The scope depends on entity type, accounting period, business activity and jurisdiction.

Which calculators can I use here?

The page includes working personal-income, sole-proprietor/freelancer profit, company provision, withholding deduction, sales-tax invoice and tax-reserve tools. Each calculates a limited estimate from the values entered.

Are VSN’s calculators official FBR tools?

No. They are VSN estimates and do not connect to or submit anything to FBR. The Tax Year 2027 individual schedule is linked to official FBR material; company, withholding and sales-tax rates must be confirmed for the specific entity or transaction.

Are figures entered in a calculator uploaded or saved?

The calculator runs in your browser and does not send the calculation inputs to a server or save them in browser storage; the values remain in the page until you clear, refresh or leave it. If you choose the separate tax enquiry form, it prepares a WhatsApp brief, so do not include tax IDs, returns, bank details or documents there.

Can you guarantee a refund, lower tax or acceptance by FBR?

No. Outcomes depend on the taxpayer’s facts, records, law and authority processing. VSN does not guarantee refunds, tax savings, return acceptance, active-taxpayer listing or notice outcomes.

Who gives formal advice or represents me?

Any formal advice, certification, appeal or representation that requires a qualified professional is handled only by the professional identified and agreed for that engagement.
