How VSN works

Clear scope, visible milestones and accountable delivery.

Custom technology projects should not begin with a vague promise. VSN structures engagements around discovery, written scope, milestone reviews, acceptance and post-launch support.

Qualify

Understand the business problem, decision makers, budget range and timing.

Discover

Map users, requirements, risks, content, integrations and measurable outcomes.

Scope

Issue a proposal or SOW covering deliverables, milestones, responsibilities and commercial terms.

Design

Validate journeys, architecture and interface direction before expensive implementation.

Build & QA

Develop in reviewable increments with security-minded engineering and acceptance checks.

Launch & Operate

Deploy, hand over agreed assets and continue with support, growth or managed operations when needed.

Commercial model

Different work needs different billing.

Fixed-scope products can use simple packages. Complex engineering, BPO and advisory work should use a proposal or SOW rather than forcing every client through the same checkout.

Productized services

Authority profiles and tightly scoped website packages can use published starting prices and clear inclusions.

Custom projects

Apps, SaaS, e-commerce and automation are normally milestone-based after discovery and written scope.

Recurring services

Social media, maintenance, BPO and managed support are normally monthly or quarterly retainers tied to capacity and service levels.

Project control

No surprise scope.

A strong engagement separates agreed deliverables from new requests. Significant changes should be documented before they affect cost or delivery.

Every SOW should define

  • Deliverables and exclusions
  • Milestones and acceptance criteria
  • Client dependencies
  • Revision limits
  • Security and access responsibilities
  • Payment schedule
  • IP ownership and handover
  • Support / warranty period
