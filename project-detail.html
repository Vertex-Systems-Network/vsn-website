VSN Metafields

A VSN-built Shopify app for managing custom product information. The Shopify App Store listing remains the source of truth for current availability, features, pricing and reviews.

About the project

Stores often need product information that does not fit neatly into standard fields. VSN Metafields is built around the need to manage that information without turning every update into a theme-editing task.

Project challenge

The useful challenge is not simply adding more fields. The product needs to fit Shopify’s platform rules, remain understandable to merchants, and support custom information without making everyday store management harder.

Key features

Structured product data

Manage additional product information through a dedicated app workflow.

Merchant-focused workflow

Keep the experience focused on store operators rather than developer-only tasks.

Platform-aware delivery

Work within Shopify’s app model, permissions and marketplace requirements.

Public verification

The live Shopify listing can be checked independently instead of relying only on a portfolio claim.

What we do not claim
