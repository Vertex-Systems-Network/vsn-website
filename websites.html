Choose a platform with a reason

Match the build to how your team works.

WordPress

Content websites, custom themes, plug-in integration and WooCommerce. We can scope work for an existing site or a new build.

Good fit when: you need a flexible content ecosystem and broad extension options.

Shopify

Online stores, themes, catalog setup, apps and integrations. Payment providers and Shopify determine account and transaction eligibility.

Good fit when: store and catalog operations are central.

Webflow

Design-led sites and visual editing in Webflow’s hosted ecosystem. Workspace and site-plan fees are set by Webflow.

Good fit when: a tightly art-directed marketing site matters.

Wix

Hosted sites using Wix tools for content, booking or commerce where its current features fit the brief.

Good fit when: a managed builder covers your requirements.

Laravel

Custom websites, portals and applications where business rules, permissions, data models or integrations need application code.

Good fit when: a visual builder cannot support the workflow.

Platform-neutral advice

We compare ongoing costs, editing, integrations, portability, performance and support before recommending a build.

How work is scoped

A written scope, not a vague promise.

Your proposal lists pages, features, content inputs, review rounds, schedule, price, launch tasks and support if selected.

Plan & structure

Define audiences, navigation, page purpose and content required from each party.

Design & build

Create agreed layouts, configure the platform and implement scoped features.

Quality & launch

Review agreed routes on common screen sizes, fix in-scope defects and coordinate launch.

Handover & support

Provide agreed access and instructions. Hosting, maintenance and new features are separate unless contracted.

Third-party costs: Domains, hosting, paid themes, plug-ins, platform plans, payment processing, stock media and advertising are separate unless your proposal includes them. VSN cannot guarantee sales, traffic, search rankings or platform approval.
