Choose a platform with a reason

Match the build to how your team works.

WordPress

Content websites, custom themes, plug-in integration and WooCommerce. We can scope work for an existing site or a new build.

Good fit when: you need a flexible content ecosystem and broad extension options.

Shopify

Online stores, themes, catalog setup, apps and integrations. Payment providers and Shopify determine account and transaction eligibility.

Good fit when: store and catalog operations are central.

Webflow

Design-led sites and visual editing in Webflow’s hosted ecosystem. Workspace and site-plan fees are set by Webflow.

Good fit when: a tightly art-directed marketing site matters.

Wix

Hosted sites using Wix tools for content, booking or commerce where its current features fit the brief.

Good fit when: a managed builder covers your requirements.

Laravel

Custom websites, portals and applications where business rules, permissions, data models or integrations need application code.

Good fit when: a visual builder cannot support the workflow.

How work is scoped

A written scope, not a vague promise.

Your proposal lists pages, features, content inputs, review rounds, schedule, price, launch tasks and support if selected.

Plan & structure

Define audiences, navigation, page purpose and content required from each party.

Design & build

Create agreed layouts, configure the platform and implement scoped features.

Quality & launch

Review agreed routes on common screen sizes, fix in-scope defects and coordinate launch.

Handover & support

Provide agreed access and instructions. Hosting, maintenance and new features are separate unless contracted.

Third-party costs: Domains, hosting, paid themes, plug-ins, platform plans, payment processing, stock media and advertising are separate unless your proposal includes them. VSN cannot guarantee sales, traffic, search rankings or platform approval.

Common questions

Website development FAQs

The final pages, platform, content responsibilities, fees and launch tasks are confirmed in your written scope.

Which platform should we use?

We compare editing needs, integrations, ongoing cost, portability and the kind of experience you need. The recommendation can be WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Shopify or a custom build when the brief requires it.

Can you redesign or migrate an existing website?

Yes. We can scope a redesign, platform migration, content move or a focused improvement to an existing site. The first step is to review what must stay, what can change and what third-party access is available.

Do you write the website content?

Content can be supplied by your team or scoped as part of the project. We can simplify and structure approved source material, but factual claims and business information still need your approval.

Are domain and hosting included?

Only when the proposal says so. Domain, hosting, premium themes, plug-ins and platform plans are normally third-party costs and are identified separately.

Will the website be mobile friendly?

Responsive behavior is part of the agreed design and build. The proposal states the browsers, devices and quality checks included before launch.

Can you help with SEO?

We can scope technical foundations, page structure, metadata and content support. Rankings and traffic are controlled by search platforms and cannot be guaranteed.

Who owns the website after launch?

Ownership, account access, source files, licences and reusable components are stated in the agreement. Handover is defined before the build begins.

Can you maintain the website after launch?

Yes, when maintenance is included or added as a separate service. New features, content work and third-party subscription costs are scoped separately.
