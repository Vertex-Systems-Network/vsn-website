How industry fit is assessed

Translate context into a delivery plan.

The discovery process clarifies what must be built, what should stay human-led, which systems are involved and how results will be assessed.

1 · Understand stakeholders

Map buyers, end users, staff, partners and decision owners, including different needs across locations and channels.

2 · Map workflows and constraints

Document the current journey, service rules, integrations, information types, approval steps, accessibility needs and operational limits.

3 · Choose the right capability

Compare website, software, app, AI, automation, marketing, managed operations or augmentation options against the actual job.

4 · Define scope and measures

Agree deliverables, owner decisions, dependencies, test criteria, budget assumptions, support needs and what evidence will show progress.
